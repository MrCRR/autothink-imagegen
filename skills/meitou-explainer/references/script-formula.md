# Script formula

The channel runs 15-25 minutes and the structure never varies. Only the subject does.

```
本期卡    4s          「本期：<标的>」 —— 一句定位，然后进钩子
钩子      7-12s        冲突问题（红）+ 一个反常识数字
结论      60-90s       先给判断，再给三个理由（观众可以随时离开）
正文      3-6 节       每节四拍：现象 -> 数据 -> 图解 -> 判断
风险      30-60s       反方、失败条件、什么情况下结论作废
如何投资  60-90s       3 条可执行动作（不是买卖点位）
收口      5-10s        一句话 + 免责声明
```

## 1. 本期卡

- On screen: `本期` kicker + the subject at 140-160px + `取自 <系列名>` in gold underneath.
- VO: one sentence that positions the subject (`这期我们聊聊市盈率，这个最常被用错的指标。`).
- 3-5 seconds. It is a title card, not a channel intro — no logo animation required.

## 2. 钩子（7-12s）

Three patterns, pick one:

| Pattern | Shape | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 冲突问题 | a question the answer contradicts | `PE 低，就是便宜吗？` |
| 反常识数字 | a number that sounds wrong | `半年跌了 30%，但它仍是防御股里最稳的` |
| 共识反转 | "everyone knows X" then break it | `都说麦当劳抗跌，直到大半年前` |

Rules:

- 红字 + 一个问题。The screen states the conflict, the VO only half-answers it.
- One visual: a gauge, a chart screenshot, a 3D object, or the whiteboard opening.
- Do not explain the answer here. The next beat does.

## 3. 结论（60-90s）

**Conclusion before evidence.** This is the channel's defining habit and the reason viewers stay.

- One sentence: `低 PE 不等于便宜，先看分母。`
- Then exactly three reasons, numbered, each 6-14 characters:
  `01 分母陷阱` / `02 行业差异` / `03 现金流验证`
- These three become the section cards. If the film has four sections, the promise has four items.

## 4. 正文：每节四拍

| Beat | Purpose | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① 现象 | what the viewer already sees | 1 headline + 1 sentence |
| ② 数据 | the number that makes it concrete | a chart or a big number, with source |
| ③ 图解 | the mechanism, drawn | whiteboard formula / diagram, or a flow of panels |
| ④ 判断 | the takeaway | 1 line, accent colour |

Rules:

- One idea per section, one number per section. Two numbers = two sections.
- The 图解 beat is where the whiteboard segment goes: a denominator, a loop, a chain of money — drawn,
  not written as bullets.
- Every number carries a source line under the card: `图：<来源> / <口径> / <时间范围>`. If the number is
  illustrative, the line says `示意图`.

## 5. 风险（30-60s）

- State the bear case honestly: `反方会说……`, then the condition that would break your conclusion.
- Screen: a red-bordered panel or a red callout on the same chart.
- This beat is what makes the analysis credible; do not skip it for time.

## 6. 如何投资（60-90s）

- 3 items, each an **action or a filter**, never a price:
  `① 现在不适合大仓位买入 / ② 但可以先建一个"小的观察仓" / ③ 一旦找对方向，反转可能比想象快`
- Numbered rows with a green check in the right column.
- No target price, no "now is the bottom", no return percentages.

## 7. 收口

- One line that restates the conclusion, in gold on the last word.
- `下期拆「现金流」` or the next-episode line.
- Disclaimer, 28px, faint: `科普内容，不构成投资建议 · 数字均为示意图`.

## Voice-over

- 4.5-5.5 字/秒. A 20-minute film is roughly 5,000-6,000 characters.
- One sentence = one subtitle line = ≤24 characters. Split long sentences at `，`.
- Speak like a person: 口语连接词 (`其实`、`而且`、`所以`、`说白了`、`这就是`) and numbers read out in
  full (`百分之三十`, not `30%`), while the screen shows `30%`.
- The **last word of a sentence lands on the picture that proves it**. Time cards to words, not to
  sentences.
- Avoid hype verbs (`暴涨`、`必赚`、`抄底`). The style is confident, not promotional.

## Worked example (the starter project, 43s)

| t | Beat | On screen | VO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 本期卡 | `本期 / 市盈率 PE` | 这期聊聊市盈率 PE，这个最常被用错的指标。 |
| 4 | 钩子 | 红色 `PE 低，就是便宜吗？` + 仪表盘 | PE 低，就是便宜吗？ |
| 11 | 节 01 | `01 分母陷阱` | 第一个，叫分母陷阱。 |
| 14 | 图解（白板） | `PE = 市值 ÷ 净利润`，红圈圈住分母 | PE 等于市值除以净利润，分母只要变小，PE 立刻变高。 |
| 26 | 数据 | 四个行业 PE 对比 + 红框 | 同样一个 PE，在不同行业是完全不同的意思。 |
| 33 | 判断 | 三步过筛 | 所以要用三步过筛：看分母、看行业、看现金流。 |
| 38 | 收口 | 结论 + 免责 | 低 PE 不是理由，会算才是。 |

## Compliance checklist

- [ ] Every number has a source and a date
- [ ] Illustrative numbers are labelled 示意图
- [ ] No target price, no buy/sell instruction, no promised return
- [ ] The bear case is stated
- [ ] 不构成投资建议 is on screen at the end
- [ ] No claim of affiliation with the channel the style is modelled on
